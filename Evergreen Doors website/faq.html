FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Evergreen Doors do?

I build client-acquisition systems for service businesses. The five services are lead lists and skip tracing, outreach campaigns (text, voicemail, email), CRM and automation setup in GoHighLevel, website creation and redesign, and organic social media content and creative. You can run them together as one system or bring me in for one piece.

Who do you work with?

I work with property and rental managers, real estate teams, home service companies, and B2B and professional services firms. My clients are service businesses that want a steady flow of new conversations.

What does the first month look like?

You can have live campaigns in about four weeks. Week 1 is strategy and audit. Weeks 2 to 3 are the build, with your lead lists, outreach copy, and CRM pipeline. Week 4 is launch, and follow-up starts running on its own. After that you get a short weekly report on activity and replies.

How does pricing work?

Builds are a fixed price, and ongoing work is billed by the hour. Websites and CRM setup are quoted up front, with half due before work starts and half at launch. Ongoing work is invoiced weekly, with a weekly cap you approve. Some projects also include a commission on new customers the system brings in. There are no retainers and no equity. I cover rates on the info call.

Do you run Facebook or Instagram ads?

No, I don't manage paid ads. I do organic content and creative for Facebook and Instagram. If you run your own ads, I can supply the creative for you to use.

Do I need GoHighLevel?

You don't need to know GoHighLevel. It's the CRM I build in, and I set it up for you.

Is your texting compliant?

I send texts through properly registered channels, with opt-out handling built in. If you want to know how that works for your market, ask me on the info call.

What happens on the info call?

It's a 20-minute call to look at your market and see whether we're a fit. It's an honest conversation, not a sales pitch. You answer a few questions first so I can prepare. When you're ready, book an info call.

Do you only work with short-term rental managers?

No. The system was built in short-term rental management, but I also work with real estate teams, home service companies, and B2B services.

Where are you based?

I'm based in Utah and work with clients remotely.
