Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated November 5, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what you choose to give us, plus basic technical data any website receives.
  • We use it to prepare for your call and talk with you about our services.
  • We don't sell your information, and this website doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Email gattlin@evergreendoorsmarketing.com any time to see, correct, or delete your information.

Who we are

This website is run by Evergreen Doors Marketing LLC, a Utah limited liability company ("Evergreen Doors," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through evergreendoorsmarketing.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website and our info calls. Work we do for clients is also governed by our written agreement with that client.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out the questions on our booking page, we collect your name, email, company name, website (optional), type of business, main market, how you currently find clients, the services you're interested in, your biggest growth challenge, and how you heard about us.

When you book a time, our scheduling provider, Calendly, collects your booking details and passes your answers along so we can prepare. If you email us or message us on social media, we receive whatever you include in that message.

Information collected automatically

When you visit any website, your browser automatically shares some basic technical details, like your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view. Our website host uses these details to deliver the site and keep it secure, and may keep them briefly in server logs. Our fonts come from Google Fonts, which receives the same basic details when the page loads.

That's as far as it goes. We don't use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies, so we don't build a profile of your visit.

How we use your information

  • To prepare for and hold your info call
  • To reply to you and follow up about the services you asked about
  • To provide services if you become a client
  • To keep this website running and secure
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

If you give us your email for a call, we may follow up by email about that conversation. You can ask us to stop at any time.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with others for their own advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as Calendly (scheduling), Google Workspace and Google Meet (email and video calls), our website host, and Google Fonts. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities or others when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • A successor business if Evergreen Doors is ever merged, sold, or reorganized, under the same protections described here.

Cookies

Cookies are small files a website can store in your browser. This website does not set its own cookies.

The scheduling calendar on our booking page is provided by Calendly and only loads after you finish the booking questions. Calendly may set its own cookies to make the calendar work, as described in Calendly's cookie notice. Google Fonts does not set cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; if you block Calendly's cookies, you can still book by emailing us.

Do Not Track. Because we don't track visitors across websites, we don't change how this site works in response to "Do Not Track" or similar browser signals.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as it's useful for the conversation and any working relationship that follows, and for as long as needed to meet legal and accounting requirements. After that we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email gattlin@evergreendoorsmarketing.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's law, and we'll honor those.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you give us, including secure (HTTPS) connections and access-controlled business accounts. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to other services, such as Calendly, Facebook, and Instagram. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll make that clear on this page.
