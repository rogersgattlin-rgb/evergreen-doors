Effective November 5, 2026. Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what you give us, plus basic technical details every website receives.
  • We use it to prepare for your call and follow up.
  • We never sell it. This site has no analytics, ad pixels, or tracking cookies today.
  • Giving a phone number is optional, and we only text you if you check the box asking us to.
  • Email gattlin@evergreendoorsmarketing.com to see, correct, or delete your information.

Who we are

This site is run by Evergreen Doors Marketing LLC, a Utah company ("we," "us"). This policy covers the website and our info calls. Client work is also covered by each client's written agreement.

What we collect

What you give us

The answers on our booking page: your name, email, phone number (optional), whether you asked us to text you, company, website (optional), type of business, market, how you find clients, the services you need, your biggest challenge, and how you heard about us. Your answers may be saved when you continue to the calendar, so we can follow up even if you don't pick a time. When you book, Calendly collects your booking details. If you email or message us, we get what you send.

What's collected automatically

Your browser shares basic details with every site you visit, like your IP address, browser, device, and pages viewed. Our website host uses them to run and secure the site and may keep them briefly in logs. Google Fonts receives the same details when our fonts load.

Today this site uses no analytics, ad pixels, or tracking cookies. If we ever add visitor statistics, we'll choose a privacy-respecting tool, name it here, and update the date at the top first.

How we use it

  • To prepare for and hold your call, and follow up about it
  • To text you about your inquiry, only if you asked us to
  • To provide services if you become a client
  • To run and secure the site
  • To meet legal and accounting requirements

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business: Calendly, Google Workspace and Meet, our website host, and Google Fonts
  • Authorities when the law requires it
  • A successor if the business is ever sold or merged, under this same policy

Phone numbers and text messages

What we collect. If you enter a phone number on our booking page, we keep the number and whether you checked the box asking us to text you.

It's optional. You don't have to give a phone number, and you don't have to agree to texts. Agreeing is not a condition of booking a call or buying any service. If you leave the box unchecked, we won't text you.

What we send. If you check the box, we may text you about your inquiry, such as scheduling your call or following up on it. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping and help. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email gattlin@evergreendoorsmarketing.com.

No sharing for marketing. We don't sell your mobile number or your text-message consent, and we don't share them with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. They go only to the providers that deliver our messages for us, and to authorities when the law requires it.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies. The Calendly scheduler, which loads only after you finish the booking questions, may set its own (see Calendly's cookie notice). You can block cookies in your browser and book by email instead.

Since we don't track you across sites, we don't respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

How long we keep it

As long as it's useful for our conversation or work together, and as long as the law requires. Then we delete it.

Your choices

Ask us any time to see, correct, or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Email gattlin@evergreendoorsmarketing.com and we'll respond, usually within 30 days. Your state's laws may give you added rights, and we'll honor them.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including secure connections and protected accounts. No system is perfectly secure.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed to children under 13. If a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Other sites

Links to Calendly, Facebook, and Instagram are covered by their own privacy policies.

Changes

We'll post any updates here and change the date at the top.
